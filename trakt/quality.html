📋 Field Population & Quality
% Populated is whether a field has any value at all; % Quality is stricter — whether that value actually clears a field-specific usefulness bar (e.g. 2+ genre tags, not just 1, or both a Rotten Tomatoes and a Metacritic score, not just one) and, for genre/subgenre/tone/subject/era, whether the field's whole value distribution is actually granular rather than dominated by one mega-value (Specificity, a normalized-entropy score comparing the real distribution to a perfectly even one) — so a field can be fully populated with every row "valid" and still score low if what's there isn't good enough to trust or isn't specific enough to be useful.
